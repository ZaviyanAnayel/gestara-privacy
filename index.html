Gestara — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

Operated by: Zaviyan

1. Introduction

Gestara ("we", "our", or "us") is a pregnancy tracking app operated by Zaviyan. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles and how it is protected.

Our core promise: your personal data stays on your phone.

2. Information Stored Only on Your Device

Gestara is offline-first and requires no account or sign-up. The following information you enter is stored exclusively on your device (in the app's private local database) and is never transmitted to us or to any third party:

  • Pregnancy dates (last menstrual period or ultrasound date) and derived week/trimester progress
  • Kick counter sessions and history
  • Contraction timer sessions (durations and intervals)
  • Weight log entries
  • Appointment reminders and checklist items (reminders are delivered by your phone's local notification system only)
  • Journal entries, including mood selections

Uninstalling the app — or clearing the app's data in your phone's settings — permanently deletes all of the above. We have no copy and no way to recover it.

3. AI Companion

Gestara includes an optional AI chat companion. When you send a message to the AI companion:

  • The message text is sent to our secure server, which forwards it to our AI provider (Groq) solely to generate a reply. The reply is sent back to your phone.
  • We do not attach your name, email, device identifiers, or any account information to these requests, because the app collects none.
  • The AI companion does not provide medical diagnosis or medical advice. Every AI answer carries a disclaimer reminding you to consult a qualified healthcare professional.

We do not use your messages for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than generating your reply.

4. Information We Do Not Collect

  • No account, name, email address, or phone number
  • No precise or approximate location
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs
  • We do not sell personal information and we do not share it with data brokers

5. Notifications

Appointment reminders use your device's built-in local notification system. Reminder content never leaves your phone.

6. Health Disclaimer

Gestara is an informational and organizational tool only. It is not a medical device and does not replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always seek the advice of your physician or another qualified health provider with any questions regarding pregnancy.

7. Children's Privacy

Gestara is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

8. Data Security

Because your entries never leave your device (Section 2), they are protected by your phone's own security (screen lock, encryption). AI chat messages in transit (Section 3) are sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

9. Your Rights

Since your personal entries exist only on your device, you are always in control: view, edit, or delete them inside the app at any time, or remove everything at once by uninstalling the app.

10. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL — the policy URL listed on the app's Play Store listing — with a new effective date.
